We are pleased about your interest in our company. Data protection is a
particularly high priority for the management of Chili Career GmbH. It is
generally possible to use the Chili Career GmbH website without providing any
personal data. However, if a data subject wishes to use particular services
offered through our website, processing of personal data may become necessary.
Where processing of personal data is necessary and there is no statutory basis
for it, we generally obtain the data subject's consent.

Personal data is always processed in accordance with the General Data Protection
Regulation (GDPR) and the country-specific data protection provisions applicable
to Chili Career GmbH.

## 3. Collection of general data and information

Each time the website is accessed by a data subject or an automated system, a
range of general data and information is collected and stored in the server log
files. This may include browser type and version, the operating system used, the
referring website, the sub-pages accessed, the date and time of access, a
truncated (anonymised) IP address, and the internet service provider of the
accessing system.

We draw no conclusions about the data subject from this general data. The
information is needed to deliver our content correctly, to optimise it, to
ensure the lasting operability of our information technology systems, and to
provide law enforcement authorities with the information necessary for
prosecution in the event of a cyberattack.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in the secure and
stable operation of the website.

## 4. Contact form and email contact

Our website provides a contact form for electronic enquiries. If a data subject
submits data through the form, it is stored automatically for the purpose of
processing or answering the enquiry. The following fields are collected:

- Name (required)
- Company (required)
- Email address (required)
- Phone number (optional)
- Type of enquiry (required)
- Message (required)

Submission requires active confirmation of a consent checkbox that is unticked by
default. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent) and, where the enquiry
concerns a prospective or existing contract, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

This data is not passed on to third parties without separate consent. It is
erased once the enquiry has been fully dealt with and no statutory retention
periods apply.

## 5. Applicant and candidate data

Chili Career GmbH provides recruitment and Executive Search services. In this
context we process personal data of applicants and candidates, including contact
details, CV data, qualifications, employment history and, where applicable,
salary expectations.

The data is processed exclusively for the purpose of assessing suitability for a
specific position and for placement with a client company. Data is transmitted to
a client company only after the candidate has been informed and has consented.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) and Art. 6 (1) (a)
GDPR (consent). Where special categories of data pursuant to Art. 9 GDPR are
involved, processing takes place solely on the basis of explicit consent.

Candidate data is erased no later than [PLACEHOLDER: e.g. 6 or 12] months after
the conclusion of the respective process, unless the candidate has consented to

## 7. Routine erasure and blocking of personal data

We process and store a data subject's personal data only for the period necessary
to achieve the purpose of storage, or where a statutory retention period applies.
Once the purpose ceases to apply or a retention period expires, the data is
routinely blocked or erased.

## 8. Rights of the data subject

Data subjects have the right to: confirmation as to whether personal data
concerning them is being processed (Art. 15); access to that data (Art. 15);
rectification (Art. 16); erasure (Art. 17); restriction of processing (Art. 18);
data portability (Art. 20); to object to processing (Art. 21); not to be subject
to a decision based solely on automated processing (Art. 22); and to withdraw
consent at any time (Art. 7 (3)).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@chilicareer.de.

## 9. Legal basis for processing

Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR serves as the legal basis where we obtain consent.
Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR applies where processing is necessary for the performance of
a contract or pre-contractual measures. Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR applies where we must
comply with a legal obligation, such as commercial or tax retention duties.
Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR covers processing necessary for our legitimate interests,
where those interests are not overridden by the interests or fundamental rights
of the data subject.

## 10. Legitimate interests pursued

Where processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, our legitimate interest lies in
conducting our business for the benefit of the well-being of all our employees
and our shareholders, and in the secure operation of our website.

## 11. Duration of storage

The criterion for the duration of storage of personal data is the respective
statutory retention period. Once that period expires, the data is routinely
erased, provided it is no longer required for the performance or conclusion of a
contract.

## 12. Statutory or contractual requirement to provide personal data

We clarify that the provision of certain personal data is in part required by law
(for example tax regulations) or may arise from contractual provisions. In some
cases it may be necessary for a data subject to provide us with personal data
which must subsequently be processed by us. In the contact form, mandatory fields
are marked as such. Without them we cannot answer the enquiry.

## 13. Existence of automated decision-making

We do not use automatic decision-making or profiling.
